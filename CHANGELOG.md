# Changelog

## 1.0.1 — 2026-11-11

Metadata only; the lexicon files are unchanged.

- Nur Hamid and Aidil Baihaqi added as creators of the lexicon (`LICENSE`, `CITATION.cff`, `.zenodo.json`,
  documentation), matching the author list of the paper.
- Zenodo DOI badge and citation added to the README.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-11

First public release, identical in content to the lexicon described in Chahyadi et al., *BIO Web of
Conferences* 262, 03011 (2026), https://doi.org/10.1051/bioconf/202626203011.

- 12,717 entries: 8,231 unchanged InSet entries, 128 revised InSet entries, 46 neutralized function words,
  2,926 new domain entries (including 2,169 bigrams and trigrams), and 1,386 non-standard forms.
- Conflict rule: a corpus score replaces the InSet score only when the entry occurs in at least 30 rated
  reviews.
- InSet-format files `positive.tsv` and `negative.tsv` added for drop-in use.
- Validated spelling-correction resources: 1,519 annotator-validated pairs; 1,956 corrections in all layers.
